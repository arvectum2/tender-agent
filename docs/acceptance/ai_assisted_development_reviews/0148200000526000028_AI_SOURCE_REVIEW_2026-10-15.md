### A1 — SiteSoft.Platform legal rights are documented in the application

Application document PARAGRAPH-0032 refers to participant compliance documents under article 31 of 44-FZ. PARAGRAPH-0034 declares a rightsholder/usage-right condition; PARAGRAPH-0035 requires documented rights to make changes to the buyer's SiteSoft.Platform. PARAGRAPH-0036, 0037 and 0038 explicitly allow THREE alternative proofs: exclusive ownership, a sufficiently broad licence from the rightsholder, or a sufficiently broad sublicense.

AI assessment: HIGH applicability/documentation risk for a bidder lacking the specified rights; not evidence that only the exclusive owner can participate, and not automatically a legally valid or invalid bidder restriction. It is an application-participant condition, NOT merely a technological preference found in the specification. Need to separately test whether the works legally justify it and what rights the buyer already has. Action: flag IP_RIGHTS_REQUIRED, retain all three alternatives, request proof before any bidder eligibility assertion.

### A2 — Separate FSTEC technical-protection licence requirement

Application PARAGRAPH-0039 explicitly demands an active FSTEC licence in the submitted application for work controlling confidentiality protection against unauthorised access and modification, citing Government Decree 79 of 3 February 2012, paragraph 4(b).

AI assessment: HIGH qualification/documentation risk, **distinct** from SiteSoft intellectual-property rights and general IT accreditation. The decree does include these categories of licensed work, but whether this specific procurement actually contains such operations, and whether requiring the licence from every bidder is justified, requires the complete specification and legal assessment. It would be unsound to call the tender unlawful without this work. Action: FSTEC_79_4B_LICENSE_REQUESTED; compare specific technical work with licensed scope and article 31 of 44-FZ; human/legal escalation.
