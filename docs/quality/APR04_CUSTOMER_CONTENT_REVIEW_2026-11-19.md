# APR04 — bounded customer draft content review

After the private archive and exact acknowledgement are rechecked, every selected customer-oriented DOCX is reconstructed independently from the retained structured terms. The exported ZIP must contain exactly the selected known names. Every decompressed inner OOXML member, including document XML, relationships, styles, metadata and other default package parts, must match the independent reconstruction byte-for-byte. Unexpected parts, duplicate entries, unexpected wording, modified metadata and unresolved placeholders are rejected. A narrow normalized text scan rejects known internal-calculation markers and control/format characters in free-text fields.

This is structural and deterministic review with a shared pinned DOCX library, not independent human review, semantic confidentiality certification, legal correctness, official status or permission to deliver. Unknown wording may still disclose information. The endpoint remains default-off, unsigned, incomplete and local-download-only. No source or profile data was imported.

## Safe original customer form release design

Do not add forms/* to the export allowlist based only on filled placeholder counts. Current maps explicitly do not prove exhaustive mapping; original DOCX/XLSX may retain hidden text, comments, formulas, metadata, embedded or external references and unknown requirements. A separate release candidate must bind the actual customer-issued source and its revision, original template SHA, exact map and applicable source requirement, current fact versions, rendered SHA, and an independent reviewer with explicit exact-byte acknowledgement. Perform package- and format-aware independent structural review; reject unknown fields and unsupported OOXML constructs. Unresolved source authenticity, application completeness and legal/semantic review remain fail-closed. No actual customer forms enabled by this change; no real submission or signature.
