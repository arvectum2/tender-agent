# APR04 — independent original form review (private pilot)

Two distinct authenticated owner/admin members in the same tenant: the creator assigns an active reviewer explicitly, and only that assigned reviewer can record acknowledgement. The selected source is the previously bounded, archived original-form candidate. Both members must remain active and eligible; the tenant-owned run and current pilot legal acknowledgement are checked for each caller. Candidate and review history are immutable at the application ORM boundary. Per-tenant candidate history is bounded to 20, with a 30-minute window for download and acknowledgement. The private feature switches default to false.

The creator and assigned reviewer can read the exact private DOCX and the historical digest/source view. No other member or tenant can retrieve the document. A reviewer must explicitly acknowledge both the candidate digest and the exact stored document SHA-256. Before recording, rebuild the candidate against the currently observed archived source and identity projection, revalidate receipt and scope, compare all decompressed OOXML parts to the stored bytes, and recheck the current principal and both participants. Different ZIP container timestamps do not change the retained exact-byte digest. Metadata, payload or evidence changes fail closed. A unique receipt prevents duplicate acknowledgement.

The browser verifies the downloaded SHA before enabling the explicit reviewer checkbox and never marks a document as ready, signed or submitted. The filename identifies it as a private unsigned form. History is historical, not a freshness assertion.

## Limitations and release gates

Separate account identifiers do not independently establish separate natural persons or genuine human/legal review. Acknowledgement is not an attestation of factual content, current procurement revision, complete mapping, completeness, confidentiality, delivery authorization or legal validity. The original form is not added to the customer export allowlist. The feature remains disabled by default; no production database migration, source import, signature, delivery or submission performed. A 30-minute expiry is not a deletion/retention SLA. Database operational retention, PostgreSQL locking/restore and concurrency acceptance, and real source/profile acceptance remain open. Synthetic browser network fixtures do not substitute for a real browser-to-API acceptance.
